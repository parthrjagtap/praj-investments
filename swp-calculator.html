What is a Systematic Withdrawal Plan?

A Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP) lets you regularly withdraw a fixed amount from your mutual fund investment — monthly, quarterly, or yearly. You choose the date; the AMC transfers the amount directly to your registered bank account. The rest of your corpus stays invested and continues to earn returns.

How Does the SWP Calculator Work?

Enter four inputs and the calculator instantly shows you projected results:

1
Total Investment Amount — the lump sum you invest upfront
2
Monthly Withdrawal — fixed amount you want to receive each month
3
Expected Annual Return — the rate at which your corpus grows
4
Investment Tenure — how many years you plan to withdraw

The calculator uses monthly compounding — each month, your remaining corpus earns interest, and then your withdrawal is deducted. This matches the standard used by mutual fund houses.

The Formula

A = PMT × [ ((1 + r/n)nt − 1) ÷ (r/n) ]
A Final value of your investment
PMT Withdrawal amount per period
r Annual rate of return
n Compounding frequency (12 for monthly)
t Investment duration in years
Example: ₹5,00,000 corpus · ₹8,000/month withdrawal · 12% p.a. · 5 years
Total invested: ₹5,00,000 Total withdrawn: ₹4,80,000 Final value: ₹2,38,441
